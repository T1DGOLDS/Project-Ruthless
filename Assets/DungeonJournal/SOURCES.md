# Forever dungeon journal facts — 2026-11-10

Source: [Wowhead's public Forever Dungeon Journal](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/dungeon-journal), retrieved directly from the public `data.page.wow.dungeonJournal` JSON embedded in each of its 30 published dungeon/wing pages. Each record retains its exact journal URL and retrieval date. The export includes 217 boss entries, 891 directed boss/item associations, 452 distinct item records and 249 dungeon/quest associations. Names, numeric identities, levels, equipment categories and ability identifiers are factual metadata. No guide prose, role strategies, screenshots, illustrations or comments are copied. The addon never downloads data.

Pickup NPC identities, pickup zones, published pickup levels, coordinates and related quest/item identifiers come from [Every Dungeon Quest in WoW Forever](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/dungeons/every-dungeon-quest-location), updated November 9. Related links do not prove an immediate prerequisite edge. Its published pickup levels are labelled as source guidance, not a guarantee of character eligibility. Class/faction masks come from the public journal's linked quest metadata; native character log/completion state decides current status. Where a guide location is absent or ambiguous, it stays absent.

[The overview](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/dungeons-overview-locations-details) lists five further Forever dungeons without published journal entries at retrieval. They appear as explicit incomplete records: The Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, Blackmaw Hold and Shaper's Terrace. Their recommended ranges are retained; bosses/maps/loot are not invented.

Entry-item preparation notes for Dire Maul West/North and Upper Blackrock Spire are original short restatements of the dungeon-quest reference's access facts. Scholomance entry options are from [its current Forever guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/scholomance-dungeon-overview-location-rewards). A party key holder or valid alternative may suffice; personal item absence is not presented as an absolute entry prohibition.

**Drop rates:** the retrieved public journal data lists associations, not probability/sample counts. Entity pages did not expose reusable rate tables through the inspected public views. There are currently no verified Forever probabilities in this export. Unknown never means 0%; Retail or Classic percentages are not substituted. The UI supports a future sourced `dropRates[itemID]` value with its source, mode and sample qualifier.

Map rendering continues to use native verified floors when available, or the already-bundled, separately licensed reference maps. These map assets are not sourced from or represented as Wowhead artwork. No later-expansion dungeon or raid data is loaded into the Forever journal. The retained historical records are not the active catalog; their original source notices remain in the data folder.

Editable facts: `facts.json`. Deterministic compiler: `python tools/build-dungeon-journal.py` (`--check` verifies the output). Raw inspected source responses and extraction proof remain in the chat workspace `work/wowhead-journal-20261110`, outside the release. Wowhead is credited as the source, not claimed as an endorsement or an addon runtime dependency.
