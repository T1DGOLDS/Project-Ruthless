# Route sources

RUI includes an original route engine. Choose a route in Map > Guides and use Chapters to review its steps. The compact tracker shows the current task, required items and the next step. Paused, stopped and unconfigured routes have explicit states. Route transitions remain deliberate selections.

The route-provider interface accepts presentation data from an explicitly selected installed guide. Provider content and progress remain owned by that source. An unavailable source does not silently become another route, and browsing never executes a step action. Protected provider callbacks wait until combat ends.

Quest browsing, native map markers and RUI pickup metadata remain independent of the route source. Known pickup requirements are checked; missing locations and gates stay uncertain. Original hub routes are not a claim of complete or optimal leveling coverage and still require live quest-by-quest review.
