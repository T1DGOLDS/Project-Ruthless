# Profession workspace — beta102

Open **Spellbook > Professions**, **Map > Professions**, or the searchable profession action in Spellbook & macros settings. The workspace shares the Spellbook window, scale, movement and appearance. The native Professions button and crafting controls remain intact.

- **Recipes:** searchable skill-sorted published and observed recipes; All, Learned, Unlearned / unknown and Vendor filters. An unobserved recipe is not asserted to be available or unlearned.
- **Leveling:** current-skill range selection, learning source, full-range material totals, average craft count and a proportional remaining estimate. Skill-up luck, perks, intermediate stock and current native difficulty can change actual quantities. Add plan uses per-craft materials and the entered number of crafts, rather than treating a full-guide material total as one recipe.
- **Sources:** recipe learning/vendor text; gathering professions also show trainers, coordinates when recorded, resources and suggested zones. All published source links are retained in the data. Reputation, faction, specialization and limited stock can still prevent a purchase.
- **Profit:** material value, direct-purchase cash for missing stock, published vendor return, auction gross, after-fee proceeds and listing margin. Output quantities and binding must be verified before an auction margin appears. Enter the market's sale fee explicitly. Deposits returned on a successful sale are not charged again; failed deposits, relisting and recipe/training unlock costs are separate.
- **Shopping:** persistent character plans; materials aggregate before owned stock is deducted. Send to AH shopping replaces the generated Profession materials list, preserving other lists. No purchase, crafting or posting happens automatically.

## Current data and limits

Reviewed 2026-11-10: **12 professions, 1,008 recipe facts, 171 crafting/gathering ranges, 1,866 item identities, 344 vendor-sourced recipes, 34 identified vendor NPCs, 229 trainer records and 39 gathering resources.** These counts describe sourced facts, not a complete database or a live verification claim. Eight crafting guides span 1–300; Fishing spans 1–300. Published Herbalism, Mining and Skinning routing currently ends at 225. Vendor location coverage, some training requirements, output quantities and exact profession recipe coverage remain incomplete. The first enchanting rod's acquisition is explicitly unconfirmed by the public source. Tailoring's two missing published craft counts remain unknown.

Native crafting observations enrich the catalogue while the character's profession is open, outside combat. RUI reads shipped candidate IDs and the native filtered list, without changing filters. Clear a native search/filter when seeking additional coverage. Only owned professions contribute learned state. Linked, guild and NPC crafting contexts are excluded where supplied by the client. Native source text, requirements, yields and mandatory reagent schematics override public facts. Ambiguous quality/currency/alternative reagent choices disable costing; optional selections are not silently included. Observation work yields after 20 recipes or approximately 2 ms; closing crafting/combat cancels it. Cached recipes remain browseable in combat. No idle polling or catalogue table allocation at login.

## Prices and accounting

All money values use copper internally. Prices are character manual overrides, then the current realm/faction's newest native auction search quote/history, then the saved full-market scan. The public library's unrelated auction averages are excluded. Quotes describe lowest observed unit prices, not executable depth quotes. Partial, old (>24 hours), insufficient observed supply, own-listing and variant limitations are labelled. Exact variant prices and reliable sales rates are not inferred from item-ID market scans. Missing prices never become zero; only an explicitly entered zero is zero.

Owned bag/bank/reagent-bank materials have opportunity value. Extra cash is separately estimated for buying missing direct ingredients, using the client's last available stock cache. Optional intermediate comparisons use this character's observed learned recipes, integer craft batches and fixed yields, with cycle/depth guards; they do not assert an optimal cross-recipe plan. Direct shopping and cash do not expand intermediate choices. Published vendor alternatives require an explicit toggle and assume stock/access at the published undiscounted price. They are not merchant purchase quotes. Services/enchants with no auction item and bound outputs have no automatic auction margin.

Prices, plans, observations and last profession stay with the character; manual prices and fees are separated by realm/faction and bounded to four market scopes. Interface profiles continue to own shared visual preferences. Native observation caches are bounded to 1,500 recipes per profession; plans to 100 and manual item prices to 1,000 per scope.

## Verification and live acceptance

`npm run test:professions` covers factual coverage, all twelve UI views, missing/zero prices, stock, batches/yields, fees, binding, history/market scope, intermediate/cycle handling, generated-list replacement, native observation bounds, secret containment and combat browsing/opening guards. `npm run test:profession-data` checks deterministic compilation. The new modules compile under actual Lua 5.1.

Required in the current client: open a learned crafting profession, then revisit the RUI catalogue; inspect learned/unlearned recipes, native source text, quantities and tooltips. Test all five views at normal/large text sizes and in combat. Capture a fresh auction quote/full scan, set its actual sale fee and compare a multi-output craft's inputs/output manually. Check BoP and service recipes, bank stock, plan reload/export and native crafting return. These checks are pending; mocks do not establish in-client rendering, taint freedom or observed performance.
